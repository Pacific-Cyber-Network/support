# 🌊 Pacific-Cyber-Network Community Support

Welcome to the **Pacific-Cyber-Network** GitHub Organization support repository! We are a community of developers focused on open-source, cybersecurity, systems engineering, and collaborative tech projects.

---

## 📌 How to Join the Organization?

We are open to developers, students, and tech enthusiasts from all around the world! To join us:

1. **Open an Issue:** Click on the **Issues** tab above and create a **New Issue**.
2. **Request Membership:** Use the title `Request to join Pacific-Cyber-Network` and drop your GitHub username in the description.
3. **Accept Invitation:** Our team (or automated workflow) will review and send you an invitation to join the organization shortly!
